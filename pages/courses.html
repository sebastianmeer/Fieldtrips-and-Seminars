Courses

I completed a set of courses that built both technical and professional skills. Python Essentials 1 introduced me to programming logic, while the Skills to Succeed Academy courses strengthened my adaptability, analysis, emotional intelligence, AI awareness, data literacy, and coding career direction.

Together, these courses taught me that an IT professional needs more than syntax. I need disciplined thinking, communication, ethical judgment, and the habit of turning lessons into working output.

Python Essentials 1 Cisco Networking Academy

Objectives: Build a solid understanding of programming principles and begin developing coding skills using Python.

What I learned: The course made programming feel approachable by breaking code into simple concepts like data types, variables, input, output, loops, and conditionals.

Impact on me: I realized that small programming concepts can combine into useful real-world programs. This strengthened my logical thinking and made coding less intimidating.

Next step: I will keep practicing Python through small projects, then move toward more advanced concepts that connect to real applications.

Lifelong Learning Skills to Succeed Academy by Accenture

Objectives: Embrace challenges, adapt to change, build confidence, and develop skills that stay relevant.

What I learned: Education is not a fixed checklist. A growth mindset is a professional survival skill in an industry where tools and standards change quickly.

Impact on me: I learned that agility is more valuable than staying locked into one identity or one technology.

Next step: I will volunteer for unfamiliar tasks and treat sudden academic or project changes as practice for real workplace adaptability.

Solving Problems Skills to Succeed Academy by Accenture

Objectives: Identify root causes, design effective resolution strategies, and navigate complex scenarios.

What I learned: The course emphasized systems thinking and warned against fixing surface symptoms while missing the real cause.

Impact on me: I now see the discovery phase as a necessary part of software work. Misunderstanding the problem can waste more effort than slow planning.

Next step: Before proposing code or architecture, I will use techniques like the Five Whys to clarify the real business or project problem.

Thinking Critically Skills to Succeed Academy by Accenture

Objectives: Fact-check information, validate data, develop sound judgment, and apply critical analysis in the workplace.

What I learned: Critical thinking is not cynicism. It means checking sources, context, bias, and evidence before accepting claims.

Impact on me: This is useful when evaluating software tools, vendor claims, system designs, or tutorials that may sound convincing but still need proof.

Next step: I will practice a trust-but-verify mindset and challenge project proposals so final solutions are stronger before deployment.

Emotional Intelligence Skills to Succeed Academy by Accenture

Objectives: Control personal reactions, stay grounded under pressure, and improve interpersonal communication.

What I learned: Emotional intelligence connects technical skill with leadership, teamwork, and user-centered design.

Impact on me: IT projects involve deadlines, scope changes, and unexpected issues. If I manage stress well, I can communicate better and support the team more effectively.

Next step: I will use the S.T.O.P.P. method during stressful debugging, exams, or group work so I can respond clearly instead of reacting impulsively.

Gen AI Fundamentals Skills to Succeed Academy by Accenture

Objectives: Understand generative AI concepts, use generative platforms, and brainstorm with AI assistance.

What I learned: Generative AI can create text, code, and ideas, but it also carries risks such as hallucinations, bias, and privacy concerns.

Impact on me: I now view AI as a powerful assistant, not a final authority. Human oversight is necessary when AI is used in real workflows.

Next step: I will improve my prompting skills while manually auditing AI output for accuracy, security, and ethical compliance.

Discovering Data Skills to Succeed Academy by Accenture

Objectives: Gather, handle, manage, translate, and use data to support decisions.

What I learned: Data needs context. The course showed the difference between raw numbers and meaningful insights that stakeholders can understand.

Impact on me: Data storytelling is important in IT because budgets, upgrades, and migrations need evidence that non-technical decision-makers can trust.

Next step: I will keep improving my database and visualization skills so my future reports can clearly support technical recommendations.

Exploring Coding Skills to Succeed Academy by Accenture

Objectives: Understand programming paradigms, draft basic scripts, and explore technology career paths.

What I learned: Programming languages are tools. A good IT professional chooses the best technology for the problem instead of treating one language as the only answer.

Impact on me: I gained a wider view of front-end, back-end, databases, APIs, and the different directions available in tech careers.

Next step: I will develop a systems-level approach by learning APIs, cloud integration, and how different parts of software communicate.

Overall, these courses gave me a balanced foundation. Python helped me begin writing code, while the professional modules taught me how to analyze problems, communicate with people, use AI responsibly, and keep learning as technology changes.
